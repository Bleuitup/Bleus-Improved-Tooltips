Commander Tooltip Audit

Every written commander tooltip checked against the game code, in vanilla and CBM dev. Each row shows today's text, what the code actually does, and a proposed rewrite in a tooltip panel at the in-game wrap width. Reply with row IDs to approve, change or drop a proposal.

Length budget

A rewrite may run at most one line longer than today's text. Lines are counted at 37 characters, the tooltip's wrap width.

Live numbers

Highlighted figures are read from the running game, like the stat row, so CBM and other balance mods show their own values.

Alien commander

Fire labels

The game has two separate fire properties. Flammable means the target takes extra fire damage (a multiplier in the code). Stops while burning means fire switches an ability off. Nothing on the marine side is flammable.
