Anyone able to confirm these three possible NS2 bugs in-game?

They came up during a code review, but haven't been verified in a live game yet. Vanilla and CBM reports are both welcome.

**1. Power Surge ending cancels a beacon despite normal power being restored**
- Use Power Surge on an Observatory while its room's Power Node is destroyed.
- Repair the node before the surge ends.
- Start a beacon late enough that its countdown overlaps the end of the surge.
Does the beacon cancel when the surge expires, even though the Observatory still has normal power? It should continue if the node remains operational.

**2. Rifle ammo disappears without giving anyone ammo**
- Keep your rifle's reserve ammo full.
- Fire and reload your pistol so its reserves are below maximum.
- Switch back to your rifle and walk over rifle-specific ammo left by another Marine's dropped rifle.
Does the ammo pickup disappear while neither weapon gains ammo? Use weapon-specific dropped ammo, not a commander's general ammo pack. As a comparison, try with both weapons' reserves full: the pickup should remain.

**3. Text/voice mutes may outlast their six-hour expiry**
The code stores a six-hour expiration time for mutes, but they may remain active beyond it while the same game client session stays open.
Has anyone noticed a player remaining text- or voice-muted more than six hours after muting them, without manually muting them again? This also needs clarification on whether the six-hour limit is intended to apply during a session or only when saved mutes are reloaded.

If you can reproduce any of these, please mention the bug number, game build, vanilla/CBM version, and other relevant mods. Include what happened versus what you expected; a short clip would help for the first two. Failed attempts to reproduce are useful too!
